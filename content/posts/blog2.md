In unit two, we learnt about image transformation and the different aspects that make up an image. For example, we learnt how grayscale and coloured pictures are recognized and stored on a computer. We understood how the different intensities of RGB form a colourful image. We could even calculate the amount of space an image or video takes up in a device based on the number of pixels and aspect ratio of the frame. By the end of unit 2, we were taught about optimization and modelling which helped in easy analysis of a problem. For example, we could plot the different train routes and timings on a graph and find solutions to efficiently time them such that they don't clash. I found it very cool that I could now understand the logic behind why images and videos take up so much storage (and not blindly blame my iphone for falsely filling up my phone space). Overall, it was a very fun unit and I enjoyed working on these topics.
