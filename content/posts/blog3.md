In unit three, we worked on failures and how to overcome them. It was an eye opening lesson that taught us how to process failures without letting it destroy our self esteem. Our own mind is our enemy as we tend to feel more shame than guilt in such circumstances. I came to a realization that we should take the high road which is slow and more thought out rather than acting on our impulses. Another topic was a case study on the knight capital bug. It was a catastrophe that lead a company to bankruptcy due to over $440 million in losses. There were many lessons from this disaster that I, as an information science student, got to understand and learn.\
While studying this unit, it dawned on me that we completely overlook the mental toll that failures can have on us and automatically give up without working on the root cause of the problem. These skills are very important as we will be facing numerous issues in our coding experiences. I myself have had a narrow mindset of easily getting discouraged whenever I come across a problem. Seeing these bugs as a learning step instead of a hinderance is extremely important especially in the information science field.
Overall, I found these topics very interesting as I went down a researching rabbit hole for each of them. It definitely taught me eye-opening lessons that I will carry forward in my computer science journey.
