In unit three, we worked on failures and how to overcome them. It was an eye openening lesson that taught us how to process failures without letting it destroy our self esteem. Our own mind is our enemy as we tend to feel more shame than guilt in such circumstances. I learnt that we should take the high road which is slow and more thought out rather than acting on our impulses. These skills are very important as we will and have already faced numerous issues in our coding experiences. Seeing these bugs as a learning step instead of a discouragement is extremely important in the information science field.
Another topic was a case study on the knight capital bug. This was a catastrophic one for the entire economy of the world. It was a disaster that resulted in the company losing over $440 million, eventually leading to bankruptcy. The bug was in the old software which sold the stocks at a low price and were bought them at a higher rate. One of the servers still had the old software after the manual update, which lead to a trade of over 4 million shares under an hour. There were many lessons that were brought about from this error which we must understand and remember.
