How to overcome failures

In unit three, we worked on failures and how to overcome them. It was an eye opening lesson that taught us how to process failures without letting it destroy our self esteem. Our own mind is our enemy as we tend to feel more shame than guilt in such circumstances. I learnt that we should take the high road which is slow and more thought out rather than acting on our impulses. These skills are very important as we will and have already faced numerous issues in our coding experiences. Seeing these bugs as a learning step instead of a discouragement is extremely important in the information science field. Another topic was a case study on the knight capital bug. This was a catastrophic one for the entire economy of the world. It was a disaster that resulted in the company losing over $440 million, eventually leading to bankruptcy. The bug was in the old software which sold the stocks at a low price and were bought them at a higher rate. One of the servers still had the old software after the manual update, which lead to a trade of over 4 million shares under an hour. There were many lessons that were brought about from this error which we must understand and remember.

Understanding DIKW, images, optimization and more

In unit one, we worked on the DIKW model which is a framework explaining how raw data is transformed into actionable insights and finally, efficient decisions. We had to make charts and I enjoyed presenting our group’s work in front of the class. We were also introduced to markdown which is a markup language that helps to create websites on wiki. I learnt that symbols like # and * could be used to edit how the text looks easily. In unit two, we learnt about image transformation and the different aspects that make up an image. For example, we learnt how grayscale and coloured pictures are recognized and stored on a computer. We understood how the different intensities of RGB form a colourful image. We could even calculate the amount of space an image or video takes up in a device based on the number of pixels and aspect ratio of the frame. By the end of unit 2, we were taught about optimization and modelling which helped in easy analysis of a problem. For example, we could plot the different train routes and timings on a graph and find solutions to efficiently time them such that they don’t clash. Overall, it was a very fun unit and I enjoyed working on these topics.
