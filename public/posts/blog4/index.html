Communicating information with clarity

May 7, 2026

In this unit, we worked with two different tools called TypeBot and Metabase. These had one theme in common- getting information accross to the user.
I enjoyed creating my own chaatbot which answered different questions based on the user’s choice. I could easily figure out the structure of this whole process since the tool was very visual and logical. In addition to this, I have had conversations with many chatbots so I had a fair idea of how to go about this whole project. One thing I found challenging though was answering questions when the user clicks more than one option. I mostly learnt this entire tool through YouTube videos and Google.
The metabase project for me was a little more challenging. I found it hard to come up with the right chart that depicted what I wanted to convey. It took a lot of trial and error and in the end, I was satisfied with my work. Since it was a very new topic, I had to watch a lot of videos to learn the logic of the tool. This project also involved group work so I enjoyed creating a cohesive and comprehensible story-line type of link with all of our charts. Overall, I did enjoy learning and doing both these projects.
