January 28, 2026

Hello, my name is Sunayana and I’m an information science major studying in Azim Premji University Bangalore. I reside in HSR Layout. I have been in Freedom International School from nursery to 12th grade. I am interested in singing, piano and badminton.
In unit one, we worked on the DIKW model which is a framework explaining how raw data is transformed into actionable insights and efficient decisions. We had to make charts and I enjoyed presenting our group’s work in front of the class. We were also introduced to markdown which is a markup language that helps to create websites on wiki. I learnt that symbols like # and * could be used to edit how the text looks easily. I found this a little challenging to learn since all these topics were very new to me. Initially, it felt unfamiliar but in the end, I learnt how to adapt to such unknown tools and languages.
